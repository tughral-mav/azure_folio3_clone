The Reporting Gap

Your Business Central Reports Should Not Slow Down Decisions

Business Central contains essential financial and operational data, but turning that data into useful insight often requires manual exports, spreadsheet consolidation, report development, and repeated requests to analysts.

When reporting is delayed or fragmented, finance teams close periods with limited visibility, sales teams react late to performance changes, and operations teams struggle to identify inventory and purchasing risks before they affect cash flow or customer service.

Folio3 helps you move from static, after-the-fact reports to interactive Power BI dashboards that keep leaders focused on the metrics that matter most.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Business Central Dashboards

What are pre-built Power BI dashboards for Dynamics 365 Business Central?
Pre-built Power BI dashboards are ready-to-configure dashboard modules designed to visualize common Business Central finance, sales, inventory, purchasing, accounts receivable, accounts payable, and operational data. They help organizations accelerate reporting compared with building every data model, measure, and report from scratch.
Can Folio3 customize Business Central Power BI dashboards?
Yes. Folio3 can configure and customize dashboards around your business structure, companies, dimensions, KPI definitions, reporting roles, and operational requirements. Pre-built dashboards provide a faster starting point, while IntelliFabric supports a scalable path for custom models, additional sources, and more advanced analytics.
Which Business Central dashboard areas can Folio3 support?
Folio3 can support dashboard needs across executive reporting, finance, budget vs. actuals, cash flow, accounts receivable, accounts payable, sales, inventory, inventory valuation, purchasing, vendor performance, supply chain, warehouse operations, manufacturing, projects, CRM-related analysis, and multi-company consolidation.
Can Power BI dashboards be embedded in Business Central?
Yes. Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central supports Power BI reports within Business Central pages, including Role Centers and other pages that include a Power BI report part. This enables users to access relevant analytics close to their daily operational workflows.
Is Microsoft Fabric required for Business Central reporting?
Not every Business Central dashboard deployment requires a full Microsoft Fabric implementation. However, Fabric becomes valuable when an organization needs stronger data integration, centralized architecture, governance, multi-source analytics, reusable semantic models, scalable reporting, or a foundation for AI and forecasting. Folio3 IntelliFabric is designed for organizations that need that more complete analytics operating model.
How long does Business Central dashboard implementation take?
The timeline depends on the number of dashboard modules, data complexity, required customizations, KPI validation, user roles, and whether additional sources are included. A pre-built dashboard approach can accelerate the initial rollout because the reporting foundation and common business logic do not need to be developed entirely from scratch.
Can dashboards combine Business Central with CRM, spreadsheets, e-commerce, or other systems?
Yes. Folio3's end-to-end analytics services are designed to integrate data from databases, data warehouses, cloud resources, files, SaaS platforms, and external services into a cohesive reporting environment. Learn more about Azure Data Analytics.
