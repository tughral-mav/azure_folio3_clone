Turn Business Central data into insight for every team

Business Central stores critical financial, operational, sales, purchasing, inventory, and project data. The challenge is turning that data into reporting that is fast to access, easy to understand, and relevant to each decision-maker.

Folio3 Azure delivers Business Central reporting dashboards designed to help teams:

  • Monitor revenue, margins, profitability, and cash position.
  • Track sales trends, customer performance, and product contribution.
  • Improve inventory visibility with stock, aging, turnover, and replenishment insights.
  • Evaluate purchasing activity, vendor performance, and open purchase commitments.
  • Review operational performance across manufacturing, warehousing, projects, and supply chain.
  • Give leadership a unified view of business performance without waiting for manual report preparation.

Rather than beginning with an empty report canvas, your team starts with purpose-built dashboards that can be configured around your organization's KPIs, reporting priorities, and user roles.
